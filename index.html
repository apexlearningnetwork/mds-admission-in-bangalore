Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is NEET MDS mandatory for MDS admission in Bangalore?+

Yes. NEET MDS is mandatory for all MDS admissions in India, including management quota and NRI quota seats in private dental colleges in Bangalore.

What is the minimum eligibility for MDS admission?+

You need a BDS degree along with a completed 1-year mandatory rotatory internship, finished by March 31st of the admission year, plus a valid NEET MDS score.

How many private dental colleges offer MDS in Bangalore?+

Bangalore has over a dozen private dental colleges offering MDS programs across 9 specializations, alongside one government college (Government Dental College, Bangalore).

What is the fee difference between government and private MDS seats?+

Government quota MDS fees typically range from ₹20,000–50,000 per year, while private/management quota seats range from ₹1.5–3 lakhs per year, depending on college and specialization.

Can I get an MDS seat through management quota without a high NEET MDS rank?+

Management quota seats have more flexible cutoffs than government quota, but a valid NEET MDS score is still mandatory. Our counselors can help match your rank to available seats.

When does KEA counseling for MDS start?+

KEA PGET counseling for MDS typically begins in October–November, following the NEET MDS result announcement in September.
